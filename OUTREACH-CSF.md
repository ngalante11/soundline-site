**Subject:** A Long Island school for CSF's first federal-credit year

Lesley,

I run Soundline, an independent, non-sectarian school for grades six through eight opening in Miller Place, Suffolk County, in September 2027, adding a grade a year through twelfth. I'm writing because CSF is the only organization in New York that is both an SGO and openly offering to be one for private schools, and because Long Island has no non-sectarian SGO at all.

That gap is the reason to talk. CSF's Long Island channel today runs through Tomorrow's Hope, which by charter serves Catholic elementary schools. Every family on the Island who would choose a secular independent school, and every middle-schooler at any school, has no scholarship organization to apply to in 2027. When New York's list goes to Treasury on February 15, that will still be true unless someone builds it. We would like CSF to be that someone, with Soundline as the first school.

The ask is specific: CSF acts as the SGO, under your SGO Services model, for a Long Island program open to legally operating independent schools. I will bring the other schools. Several small independent and progressive schools in Suffolk face the same problem, and a multi-school program satisfies the more-than-one-school rule from day one. On the donor side, we are already building the ask into founding enrollment: every family we enroll comes with neighbors who can give $1,700 at no net cost, and the first hundred of those is a real fund. We would rather route that money through an SGO with twenty-seven years of compliance than invent one.

What we have in hand: founders who wrote New York's statewide virtual-school framework and run STEAM programs in libraries across the Island, a tuition structure, a financial-aid line in the first budget, and a willingness to run whatever verification platform and reporting CSF uses. What we need from CSF is a conversation before December 1, so that whatever New York asks SGOs to file in January, Soundline is already in the submission.

Could we have thirty minutes in the next two weeks? I can come to 38th Street.

Nicole Galante
Founder, Soundline
Miller Place, NY

## Notes on the draft

- **Lead with the gap, not the school.** CSF has a VP whose job is the federal credit and a CEO who lobbied Albany for the opt-in. What they lack is a Long Island story that isn't Catholic. The email hands them one.
- **"I will bring the other schools" is the strongest sentence.** A single-school ask looks like a favor; a program looks like growth. Have two or three Suffolk independent schools ready to name on the call, even informally. The §25F rule that an SGO must serve students at more than one school makes this structural, not decorative.
- **The donor paragraph is the lever.** Every other school approaching CSF asks for scholarships. Soundline is offering donors. Keep it, but keep the phrasing "route that money through an SGO," not "designated to Soundline": school-designated giving is unresolved in the regulations and CSF's counsel will notice.
- **"Twenty-seven years" assumes CSF's 1998 founding.** Verify before sending, or cut the number.
- **Do not mention the February 15 deadline as pressure on CSF.** They know it better than we do. It is there once, as context.
- **If Lesley is unreachable in a week,** the same email goes to Darla directly with one changed first line.
