How it works

Grouped by skill, not age. Nobody moves on until they've got it, and nobody waits once they have.

Real work, finished, shown to someone. A robot, a documentary, a song for the ensemble. Every six weeks, a defense. No slides.

Fridays, the whole school builds one thing. At 2:00 someone presents and something is performed. Families welcome.

Who's building it

Nicole Galante, founder. Twenty years helping schools get better; led the team that wrote New York's framework for virtual schools.

Adam King, lead guide. Runs instrument labs and escape rooms for libraries across Long Island.

Gina Dimichino, operations. Thirty-five years teaching North Shore kids to dance. Izzy Galante, academic systems.

What it costs

$1,500 a month, paid over twelve. Founding families pay $1,350, and that rate holds through twelfth grade. The three-day Collaborative for homeschooling families is $900, or $810 founding. No fees on top.

We don't have a lease yet. The $500 that reserves a seat sits with an independent escrow agent, not with us, and comes back in full until the day one is signed.

See it before you hear about it.
