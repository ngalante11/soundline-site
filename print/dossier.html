An independent, non-sectarian school for grades 6–8 in Miller Place, New York.

Opening September 2027 on the North Shore of Long Island, adding a grade a year through twelfth. Prepared for organizations seeking a place on New York’s first federal scholarship list.

The school

In-person, five days a week, 8:30 to 3:00, with extended day to 5:30. Students are grouped by skill rather than age across the three grades: two hours of daily practice, four hours of making, a six-week Quest, the arts, and a Showcase every Friday. Each student has the same advisor from the day they arrive.

Independent and non-sectarian. New York does not license private schools; Soundline will file for its NYSED nonpublic-school registration and BEDS code in spring 2027, effective July 1, 2027, and teaches everything New York expects.

Families and eligibility

First-year cohort of 30 to 36 students from Miller Place, Rocky Point, Mount Sinai, Sound Beach, Shoreham and Port Jefferson. Founding enrollment opens November 12, 2026; seats are reserved with $500 held by an independent escrow agent, refundable in full until the lease is signed.

At 300% of the Nassau–Suffolk area median, the federal ceiling sits near half a million dollars for a family of four. Nearly every Soundline family will qualify. Soundline will run whatever income verification platform and enrollment reporting its SGO partner uses, and carries a financial-aid line in its first budget.

Donors

The federal credit is built into founding enrollment from the first conversation: every family that joins is asked to bring neighbors who can give $1,700 at no net cost. Twenty donors is a scholarship. A hundred is a $170,000 fund, raised from people who are not out a cent.

Soundline will direct that campaign to its SGO partner rather than form its own, and will describe donor preference for the school only as the partner’s counsel permits.

Legal and documents

Legal entity, EIN, formation documents, tuition schedule, W-9 and nondiscrimination policy available on request. Tuition is published in full before any family is asked to commit, and deposits never touch the school’s own account before a lease exists.

Other Long Island independent schools face the same gap. Soundline will convene them so that a New York program serves students at more than one school from its first year.

The ask

List Soundline as a participating school in your New York program for the 2027 credit year, and tell us in writing what you need from a school that opens in the fall of that year, and by when. We will have it ready before you ask twice.
