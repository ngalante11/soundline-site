Depth with direction.

Is your kid doing fine on paper and quietly checked out?

Or bored, waiting for everyone else. Or working hard, and nobody's noticed. Middle school is three years and they go fast. We're opening a school for exactly those three years.

What Soundline is

A new independent school for grades six through eight, opening September 2027 in Miller Place and adding a grade a year through twelfth. Ten to twelve students in a grade. Two hours of practice every morning: math, reading, writing, at a table with a guide. Four hours of making: seminar, lab, studio, the Quest. One advisor who stays with your child for all three years. Every adult in the building knows your kid by name.

Students are grouped by what they can do, not by age. Nobody moves on until they've got it, and nobody waits once they have. Every six weeks they make something and defend it in front of people who matter to them. That folder of work goes with them wherever they go next.

What it costs, in plain numbers

$1,500 a month, paid over twelve. Founding families pay $1,350, and that rate holds through twelfth grade. No fees on top. Real financial aid in the first budget. A new federal scholarship tax credit begins January 2027, the same school year we open; everything we know is at soundlineschool.com/cost.

The part you'd want to know

We don't have a lease yet. We announce the building the day it's signed, not before. The $500 that reserves a founding seat sits with an independent escrow agent, not with us, and comes back in full, for any reason, until that day.

See it before you hear about it

Free Saturday builds for ages ten to thirteen, run by Adam King, who has run instrument labs and escape rooms in libraries across Long Island for years. Drop off at ten. Come back at 11:15 and watch your own child explain something they made to a room.
