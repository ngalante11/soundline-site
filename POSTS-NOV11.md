## Set two — the town groups, from your personal profile

**Read the group rules first.** Most North Shore town groups restrict promotion, and most will allow a free community event at the public library if you ask the admin rather than discover the rule afterwards. One message to each admin, today.

**Space them out.** Four near-identical posts from one profile inside an hour is what Facebook's spam heuristics are built to catch. One group a day, or two a day at most, and vary the wording — which is why these are four different posts and not one post four times.

### Miller Place

> Hi neighbours — I'm opening a school here.
>
> Soundline is a small middle school for grades six through eight, opening in Miller Place next September. Three grades, eight to ten students in each, grouped by what they can actually do rather than by age.
>
> If you've got a kid in that range, we're doing a free talk at the Port Jefferson Free Library on Wednesday the 11th at 7:00. Come and ask the hard questions — genuinely, that's what the evening is for.

>
> Free information night at the Port Jefferson Free Library, Wednesday the 11th at 7:00. No sign-up, nothing to buy. Come and ask whatever you want.

## One thing to do in every group

**Answer every comment yourself, within the hour.** A local group post with eight replies from the person who wrote it outruns the same post with none, both in the feed and in how it reads. Comments are also where the phone calls come from — anyone who asks a real question in public gets a reply in public and a message in private offering fifteen minutes.
