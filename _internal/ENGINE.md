# The recruitment engine

Working notes, 12 Nov 2026. Companion to `PLAN-35.md` (the diagnosis), `CALL-GUIDE.md` (the conversation) and `EMAILS-NOV.md` (the three emails). This file is the operating system that makes those cycle week after week instead of once.

**The ledger:** [Soundline recruitment pipeline](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dAXEYQd4RiLOkb2xkurleGG2_O1GrT9dHbGmm_vX2Ls/edit) (Google Sheet). Every family is one row. If it isn't in the sheet, it didn't happen.

---

## 0. What an engine is, and what we had

PLAN-35 §0 found the real problem: no list, and no mechanism that fills one. Since then the pieces have been built one at a time: a survey, an RSVP page, a call guide, three emails, nine posts, two ad tracks. Each is good. None of them feeds the next on its own, and nothing counts.

An engine is three loops that hand off to each other, one ledger they all write to, and a weekly rhythm that reads the ledger and reallocates effort. Nothing below is new work. It is the existing work, connected, with a scoreboard.

```
  SOURCE  ───►  SOUND  ───►  COMMIT
  institutions  the call     reserve → deposit → enrolled
  and posts     (CALL-GUIDE) ($500 escrow, $2,500, September)
  produce names produce fit  
                + next date       every loop asks: "who's one other family?"
                + a referral ─────────────────────────────────────────► back to SOURCE
```

## 1. The ledger

Six tabs. Three you write in daily, two you write in weekly, one you read.

| Tab | What goes in it | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline** | One row per family. Stage, source, their words verbatim, the objection, the next step *with a date*, who owns it. | The same day a name arrives, from any channel. |
| **Touches** | One row per conversation: call, email, room, coffee, build. Minutes, who talked more, what they said, what was agreed, whether the referral was asked. | Within two hours of the call, as CALL-GUIDE already says. |
| **Sources** | One row per institution (library, dance studio, Facebook group, PTA, co-op). The one specific ask, when it was made, the answer, and how many families it produced. | Thursday, ask day (§3). |
| **Events** | Information nights, Saturday builds, scholarship talks. Who came, how many conversations it booked. | The night of. |
| **Scoreboard** | Reads everything above. Nothing is typed here except the blue targets. | Monday, read only. |
| **Next steps** | One dated row per task, with an owner, from today to the busing forms in March. Tick Done; late rows turn red; filter by owner for one person's list. The counts at the top are the Monday agenda. | Monday, and whenever a task is done. |
| **Lists** | The dropdown values. Edit here to add a stage, a source kind or an owner. | Rarely. |

Two rows marked "Example parent (delete this row)" show the format. Delete them once the first real family is in.

**The one rule.** Every open row has a next step and a date. The sheet turns a row red the day that date passes. A red row is the first thing Monday looks at, and the only acceptable fixes are a new date or a move to Lost or Not a fit. "Following up" is not a stage.

### The stages, and when a row moves

| Stage | A family is here when… |
|---|---|
| New | We have a name and a way to reach them, from anywhere. |
| Survey done | The form came in. (Most rows start here.) |
| Call booked | A date and time are agreed, not offered. |
| Sounded | The call happened and is logged in Touches. The row now has their words and their objection. |
| In a room | They came to an information night, a build or a coffee. |
| Reserved | $500 in escrow. |
| Deposit | $2,500. |
| Enrolled | First day, September 2027. |
| Lost | They said no, or went silent after three touches. Log the reason in Objection. |
| Not a fit | We said no. Also logged, so the pattern shows. |

A row can skip stages (a Collaborative family may go Sounded → Reserved in a week). It cannot sit in one without a date.

## 2. The three loops, and who runs each

### Source: institutions produce names
*Owner: whoever holds the relationship. Gina for dance, Adam for libraries, Nicole for the town groups and PTAs.*

The Sources tab is prefilled with the institutions PLAN-35 §4.4 named. The ask is written in the row and it is always one concrete thing with a date on it: a flyer on the counter and the 12 December date; a line in the newsletter; a Saturday build on the library calendar. "Please share" is not an ask.

**Quota: six asks a week** (the figure PLAN-35 set for the week of 11 November; it stays until the Sources tab says yes more than it says waiting). Every ask gets a row before it is made, and "Asked on" is filled the day it is. The Scoreboard counts them.

**What produces names fastest, in order,** and the scoreboard's "Where families come from" table is how we find out we were wrong:
1. Homeschool co-ops and groups (already out of the system; the Collaborative by the term is the whole pitch)
2. Gina's dance families (a thirty-five-year list with her name on it)
3. Adam's library circuit, as Saturday builds and scholarship talks
4. The four town Facebook groups, from a personal profile, one a day
5. Paid, geo-fenced, small; the pixel now carries the source tag, so the spend finally teaches something

### Sound: names become conversations
*Owner: Nicole. Adam or Izzy for the academic second call.*

This is `CALL-GUIDE.md`, unchanged. What the engine adds is the count: **twenty calls held before 14 January.** The Scoreboard divides what's left by the weeks left and shows calls per week. On 12 November that is about two a week, which is nothing; it will not stay nothing if calls slip.

Every call writes two rows: the Touches line (verbatim, who talked more, next step, referral asked) and the Pipeline update (stage, next step date). The verbatim column is the asset. Ten of them are the December ad copy and the answer to which FAQ carries the weight.

Every call ends with the referral question. Column R says whether it was asked. The Scoreboard counts how often. If that number is low, the problem is not the families.

### Commit: conversations become seats
*Owner: Nicole, with Gina on the paperwork.*

The escrow sentence does the work here and it is already in every email and the call guide. What the engine adds is **a date on every open row** between Sounded and Reserved. A family that has been sounded and has no next date is a family we are hoping about.

Melt is real (PLAN-35 assumes 15% between deposit and first day). Reserved and Deposit rows keep a next step too: the building announcement, the Family Council invitation, the busing form by 1 March, the IEP request by 1 June.

### The referral loop
Every founding family gets a link of their own: `soundlineschool.com/?r=<surname>`. Anyone who arrives through it is tagged `referral:<surname>` on the pixel, in the form (once the code question exists, §4) and in the subject line of a call request. Hand it over on the Reserved call, with the sentence from CALL-GUIDE §7: *who's one other family whose kid you'd want in the room with yours?* Ten founding families with a link each is the only list a pre-launch school can have that behaves like a current-parent network.

## 3. The week

Thirty minutes on Monday, two hours on Thursday, two-hour replies every day. Everything else is calls.

**Monday, 30 minutes, Scoreboard open.** Read it in this order and stop at the first thing that is wrong.
1. Red rows in Pipeline. Give each a new date or move it out. Nothing else happens until there are none.
2. Calls a week to get there. If it is above three, the week is calls and nothing else.
3. Next steps due in the next 7 days. Put them in the calendar now.
4. Where families come from. Which source produced names that reached a call? Thursday's six asks go to that kind of institution. The channel that produced nothing in two weeks gets dropped, however much we like it.
5. Institutions that said yes, against asked. Below one in three, the ask is wrong, not the institution.

**Every day: two hours.** A reply within two hours is a conversation; next day is a transaction (EMAILS-NOV). Every reply to any of the three emails, every form, every comment in a town group goes into Pipeline the same day with a next step. The inbox is not the ledger.

**Thursday: ask day.** Six institutional asks, each one a Sources row first. Thursday because the answer comes Friday and the flyer goes up before the weekend.

**Friday, 10 minutes.** Touches complete for the week? Every call has its verbatim line? Events updated? Then close the laptop.

## 4. How the site feeds the ledger

Three doors, all now carrying the same source tag.

| Door | Page | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Survey | `/survey/` | The default. A few minutes; puts them first in line. |
| RSVP | `/rsvp/` | Event pages. "Send me the number" with no room required. |
| **Talk** | `/talk/` | **New today.** Fifteen minutes about their child. The phone is the channel that converts and until now the site had no way to ask for one except a bare email address. Linked from the Join page. |
| **Build** | `/build/` | **New today.** The Saturday builds, with dates in `build/builds.js` (one list, shared with the two flyers in `print/`). Past dates drop off by themselves. The home and Join pages point here now instead of at November 11. |

**Source tags.** Any link into the site can carry a tag, and the site remembers it for the visit and writes it onto all three doors:

| Tag | Example | Use it on |
|---|---|---|
| `?s=` | `soundlineschool.com/?s=flyer-pjlibrary` | Every flyer's QR code, every newsletter line, every group post. One tag per placement, so a counter in a dance studio and a line in a PTA bulletin can be told apart. |
| `?r=` | `soundlineschool.com/?r=galante` | The referral link each founding family gets. |
| `?a=` | `?a=h`, `?a=p` | The two ad tracks, as before. |

The tag reaches the pixel on every event, the subject line of every call request (`Fifteen minutes about my kid (flyer-pjlibrary)`), and the Google Form itself once one thing is done:

> **Set `FORM_SOURCE_ENTRY` in `site.js`.** Add a short-answer question to the survey form, "How did you hear about us? (code)", use the form's *Get pre-filled link* once to see the question's id (`entry.1234567890`), paste it into `site.js`. From then on every response arrives with its source filled in, and the Pipeline "Source detail" column is a copy-paste from the form's own sheet rather than a guess.

**Two settings on `/talk/`, both optional.** `TALK_PHONE` publishes a number families may call directly (empty publishes nothing; the decision whether a founder's mobile goes on the public site is yours, not the page's). `TALK_FORM_URL` embeds a short form (name, child's grade, number, times that work) under the card, for parents who won't open their email.

**Still unset:** `ANALYTICS_DOMAIN` in `site.js`. The pixel measures ad traffic; Plausible would measure everything else, including which flyer tag produced which door. It is one line once the Plausible site exists.

## 5. The numbers, and how to read them when they are bad

The Scoreboard's blue targets are PLAN-35 §4, unchanged: 310 surveys, 125 in a room, 41 deposits, 35 enrolled, and 20 calls by 14 January. §0 of the same file already said the 310 will not come from cold channels. The target stays on the board anyway, because the gap between it and reality is the argument for every Thursday ask.

When the Scoreboard says behind, the order of moves is fixed and it is not "spend more":
1. **Red rows first.** Behind with red rows is a discipline problem, not a demand problem.
2. **Calls per week up.** Every open row between Survey done and Sounded gets the EMAILS-NOV follow-up: *two or three times that work this week and I'll call you.*
3. **Asks toward whatever produced calls.** The source table says where.
4. **Then paid**, retargeting `cost.html` and `/talk/`, never cold.

The one number that is allowed to go down is "Families in the pipeline", when Monday moves stale rows to Lost. A smaller true pipeline beats a larger imagined one.
