## 3. The release: the scholarship story

For the TBR editor, LongIsland.com, and anyone who asks "why is this news."

> **FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**
> **Miller Place, NY, [date]**
>
> **New federal scholarship credit starts in January. On Long Island, families outside religious schools have nowhere to apply.**
>
> A federal tax credit for K-12 scholarships takes effect on January 1, 2027. A taxpayer who gives to a qualifying scholarship granting organization receives a dollar-for-dollar federal credit of up to $1,700, and the organization pays tuition scholarships to families earning up to 300 percent of area median income. On Long Island that ceiling is well above most household incomes in Suffolk County.
>
> New York announced on October 9 that it intends to take part. The state's list of approved scholarship organizations is due to the IRS by February 15, 2027.
>
> As of this month, every organization preparing to be listed in New York serves a religious school network. For a Long Island family choosing an independent, non-sectarian school, and for any family whose child is in middle school, there is no organization to apply to.
>
> Soundline, an independent, non-sectarian school for grades six through eight opening on Long Island’s North Shore in September 2027, is working to close that gap before the February deadline, in partnership with national scholarship organizations and other Long Island independent schools.
>
> "The credit costs a donor nothing. Forty neighbors giving $1,700 is a $68,000 scholarship fund raised out of federal tax bills, not out of anyone's pocket," said Nicole Galante, Soundline's founder. "That money is going to flow somewhere in 2027. Right now, on Long Island, none of it can flow to a child at a secular independent school, and none of it can flow to a middle schooler at all. We'd like to change that in time."
>
> Soundline is holding free Saturday builds for children aged ten to thirteen through the winter, and a public session on the scholarship credit on December 12. Details at soundlineschool.com/build and soundlineschool.com/cost.
>
> **About Soundline.** Soundline is an independent, non-sectarian school for grades six through eight, opening in Miller Place, Suffolk County, in September 2027 and adding a grade each year through twelfth. Ten to twelve students in a grade; two hours of daily practice in math, reading and writing; four hours of making. Founded by Nicole Galante with Adam King, Gina Dimichino and Izzy Galante. soundlineschool.com.
>
> **Contact:** Nicole Galante, hello@soundlineschool.com, [phone if you want it printed]

Before sending: confirm the "every organization preparing to be listed in New York" sentence is still true against `reports/New York SGO partners for Soundline.md`, and soften to "nearly every" if anything has changed. Do not name CSF, ACE, AFC or American GOAL while those conversations are open.
