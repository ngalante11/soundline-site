# National and multi-state SGOs organizing for IRC 25F, and whether any plan to operate in New York

Research date: 29 October 2026. Scope: national / multi-state scholarship granting organizations (SGOs) positioning for the federal scholarship tax credit (IRC §25F, enacted in P.L. 119-21 "One Big Beautiful Bill Act", July 2025; credit effective 1 January 2027), with emphasis on New York and on what a new independent private middle school in Miller Place (Suffolk County, Long Island) would need to do to be paid by them.

Method note: web search summaries were the main source; most organization websites (acescholarships.org, scholarshipfund.org, afcscholarshipfund.org, federalregister.gov, home.treasury.gov, the74million.org, nyscatholic.org, agudah.org, acsi.org, ejewishphilanthropy.com) could not be fetched directly from this session (network allow-list), so organization-page details are reported from search-engine extracts of those pages and from secondary coverage. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer was also unreachable, so 501(c)(3) status/EINs are reported only where a source stated them.

## Regulatory baseline that every answer below depends on (as of 29 Oct 2026)

### Takeaway
Treasury/IRS issued proposed and temporary §25F regulations on 1–2 October 2026 that expressly allow one SGO to be listed in many states ("multistate SGO") as long as it is "located in" each state and keeps a separate §25F account per state; 30 states are on the IRS participating list as of 22 October 2026 and New York is not yet on it despite the 9 October 2026 announcement; state SGO lists for 2027 are due to the IRS by 15 February 2027.

### Cited Findings
- The IRS "Participating states" page, updated 22 October 2026, lists 30 states for 2027 (AL, AK, AR, CO, FL, GA, ID, IN, IA, KS, KY, LA, MS, MO, MT, NE, NV, NH, ND, NC, OH, OK, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, VA, WV, WY). New York is not on the list. The page says a state must elect to participate and "provide the IRS with a list of participating SGOs" before donors can claim the credit — [IRS participating states](https://www.irs.gov/government-entities/federal-state-local-governments/federal-scholarship-tax-credit-fstc-participating-states)
- Notable absentees from that list: Arizona, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Michigan, California, New Jersey and New York. K-12 Dive (Oct 2026) names California, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Michigan and North Carolina as not having opted in as of 14 Sept 2026 (North Carolina has since appeared on the IRS list) — [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/irs-releases-proposed-school-choice-rule-Education-Freedom-Tax-Credit/831680/); New Jersey still being urged to opt in on 22 Oct 2026 — [Jewish Link](https://jewishlink.news/federal-scholarships-could-transform-tuition-affordability-new-jersey-should-opt-in/)
- Proposed regulations (REG-117199-25) and temporary regulations were published in the Federal Register on 2 October 2026 — [Federal Register proposed rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/02/2026-20264/federal-scholarship-tax-credit); [Federal Register temporary rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/02/2026-20277/federal-scholarship-tax-credit); Treasury press release — [Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0641)
- Multistate SGOs: "An SGO may appear on more than one participating state's list if it is located in each state and maintains a separate Section 25F account for each state"; donors may direct a gift to a specific state's account — [BakerHostetler](https://www.bakerlaw.com/insights/tax-credit-for-donations-to-scholarship-granting-organizations/); [KPMG June 2026 preview summary](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/06/treasury-previews-sgo-tax-credits.html); the 85% "operational safe harbor" is tested separately per state account — [RubinBrown](https://www.rubinbrown.com/insights-events/insight-articles/new-federal-scholarship-tax-credit-what-donors-and-scholarship-organizations-need-to-know/)
- "Located in" a state: Treasury's June 2026 preview said an SGO is located in a state if it is authorized to do business there and is subject to the state's general charity laws (transparency, accountability, fraud prevention) — [Treasury preview PDF](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/136/Preview-of-Forthcoming-Guidance.pdf); secondary summaries of the October rules say no physical office or in-state staff is required — [Scholar Street summary](https://scholarstreet.org/news/treasurys-proposed-federal-scholarship.html); [eftccredit how-to-start-an-SGO](https://eftccredit.com/learn/how-to-start-an-sgo)
- Scholarships may only be awarded to students residing in the state that listed the SGO (exceptions for military families and Indian lands); donors can live anywhere — [AFC Scholarship Fund, "Donations cross state lines, scholarships don't"](https://afcscholarshipfund.org/articles/donations-cross-state-lines-scholarships-dont/); [RubinBrown](https://www.rubinbrown.com/insights-events/insight-articles/new-federal-scholarship-tax-credit-what-donors-and-scholarship-organizations-need-to-know/)
- Participating states "may not impose additional operating restrictions on SGOs beyond what's allowed by the federal law… they can't restrict the type of school that students with scholarships can attend"; but an individual SGO "may narrow its own focus and adopt more stringent requirements" — [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/irs-releases-proposed-school-choice-rule-Education-Freedom-Tax-Credit/831680/); [eftccredit homeschool-by-state](https://eftccredit.com/learn/homeschool-eligibility-by-state)
- SGO requirements: 501(c)(3) public charity (not a private foundation); scholarships to at least 10 students who do not all attend the same school; at least 90% of income spent on scholarships; household income verification (≤300% of area median gross income); no earmarking for a specific child; annual financial and program audits provided to the state — [Stateline, 1 Oct 2026](https://stateline.org/2026/10/01/feds-provide-guidance-on-new-federal-k-12-scholarship-tax-credit-program/); [Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/insights/insights/proposed-federal-school-choice-regulations-what-private-schools-need-to-know); [DFER FSTC explainer](https://dfer.org/fstc/)
- Timeline: advance elections via Form 15714 by 1 January 2027; state SGO lists ("perfected list") due to IRS by 15 February 2027; IRS then publishes a combined SGO list by state on irs.gov; a state that misses 15 Feb 2027 effectively prevents any SGO in that state from qualifying for 2027 — [EdChoice, rules are here](https://www.edchoice.org/2026-the-federal-tax-credit-rules-are-here-heres-what-we-know/); [eftccredit NY news](https://eftccredit.com/news/new-york-governor-hochul-opts-in-may-2026); [Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/insights/insights/proposed-federal-school-choice-regulations-what-private-schools-need-to-know)
- As of late July 2026 "no state had opened an SGO application process"; AFC Scholarship Fund reported that of 30 opted-in states only one had sent Treasury a list — [AFC Scholarship Fund article](https://afcscholarshipfund.org/articles/thirty-states-said-yes-only-one-has-sent-treasury-a-list/); eftccredit's directory (Oct 2026) reports "20 organizations are on an official state §25F list so far, from 1 state" — [eftccredit SGO directory](https://eftccredit.com/sgos)
- "School" is defined by reference to IRC §530 (Coverdell): a K-12 public, private or religious school "as determined under state law"; qualified expenses follow the Coverdell definition — [AFC Scholarship Fund homeschool article](https://afcscholarshipfund.org/articles/eftc-homeschool-school-definition-state-law/); [Pioneer Institute FAQ](https://pioneerinstitute.org/federal-scholarship-program-faq/)
- Public comment on the proposed regulations runs through the end of November 2026 — [EdChoice, rules are here](https://www.edchoice.org/2026-the-federal-tax-credit-rules-are-here-heres-what-we-know/)

### Inferences
- Because an SGO need only be "authorized to do business" in New York and subject to NY charity law (i.e., registered with the NY Attorney General's Charities Bureau) to be "located in" NY, every national SGO below could in principle be placed on New York's list without opening a NY office. The gating item is New York's own election (Form 15714 by 1 Jan 2027) and whatever state process New York uses to compile its list by 15 Feb 2027 — neither of which has been published as of 29 Oct 2026.
- Because states cannot restrict school type, a secular independent middle school in NY cannot be excluded by the state; it can only be excluded by an SGO's own self-imposed focus (e.g., Jewish-only, Catholic-only, Christian-only, or "partner school" networks).

### Gaps
- Could not read the Federal Register text directly; the "no physical office" reading of "located in" comes from secondary/vendor summaries, not the rule text.
- No source describes New York's state-level SGO application process, the responsible agency, or any deadline for NY SGOs to apply.

## Key Question 1: Which established state-program SGOs are going multi-state for the federal credit?

### Takeaway
The clearest multi-state movers among established SGOs are ACE Scholarships (announced 9 Oct 2026 it will expand to all 50 states and has a New York landing page), American GOAL (Georgia GOAL's federal arm, marketed as "available in all 50 states" and already signed by three dioceses outside Georgia), ACSI's Children's Tuition Fund (five-state operator that explicitly celebrated New York's opt-in and will serve non-ACSI schools), CEN SGO (Ohio's Christian Education Network going national, Christian schools only), Children's Scholarship Fund (NYC-headquartered national network offering "SGO services" to partners), and the Catholic diocesan aggregators (Reficio/"American Catholic Education Fund" and the Aequitas/ACE "Catholic Education Fund"). Step Up For Students is Florida-only; Empower Illinois, Big Shoulders, Pennsylvania SOs and Arizona STOs are stranded in non-participating states for 2027; Sagamore's Scholarships for Education Choice is Indiana-focused.

### Inferences
- For a secular independent middle school on Long Island, the established operators most likely to list any state-recognized private school (rather than a religious network) are ACE Scholarships, American GOAL (which names "independent schools"), and Children's Scholarship Fund. ACE's "physical K-12 private school" rule and funds-limited partner slots are the main constraints.
- The "all 50 states" claims by ACE and American GOAL are fundraising-side claims; on the scholarship side they still need to be placed on New York's list by 15 Feb 2027, which requires NY to run a listing process that does not yet exist publicly.

## Key Question 2: Which brand-new national SGOs have been incorporated specifically for §25F since July 2025?

### Takeaway
At least four purpose-built national SGOs have launched since July 2025: the AFC Scholarship Fund (American Federation for Children + Odyssey, 28 Jan 2026), the Opportunity Fund for Jewish Education (OU/Teach Coalition/JFNA/Prizmah, Sept 2026, Jewish day schools only), Step Up, Step Further Scholarship Fund (Florida-only), and Reficio/American Catholic Education Fund (Catholic-only); the Invest in Education ecosystem launched a convening body, the National K-12 Opportunity Association (26 Oct 2026), rather than an SGO. ## Key Question 4: What do these SGOs require of a participating school, and could a school opening September 2027 register in early 2027?

### Takeaway
Federal rules put compliance on the SGO (income verification, audits, 90% spend, no earmarking) and let each SGO add its own school criteria; the published school-onboarding steps so far are lightweight (ACE: email basic details; AFC: interest form → eligibility check → co-branded portal; FACTS-based SGOs: school verifies enrollment), but no SGO has published rules on new or not-yet-operating schools, and because "school" is defined by state law a NY school will need to exist as a NYSED-recognized nonpublic school (BEDS code) by the time scholarships are paid.

### Cited Findings
- Federal/Treasury school-side facts: participating states cannot restrict school type; SGOs may narrow focus (e.g., STEM, performing arts) and "adopt more stringent requirements" but may not discriminate in violation of the Fifth or Fourteenth Amendments — [Informed Choice/EdChoice summary](https://informedchoice1996.substack.com/p/the-federal-scholarship-tax-credits); [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/irs-releases-proposed-school-choice-rule-Education-Freedom-Tax-Credit/831680/); SGOs must verify family income and keep funds from disqualified persons; streamlined verification for foster, needs-based-service and certain tutoring/disability cases — [EdChoice, rules are here](https://www.edchoice.org/2026-the-federal-tax-credit-rules-are-here-heres-what-we-know/)
- Venable (Aug 2025): "participation in the scholarship program will not subject independent schools to federal compliance obligations as a recipient of federal financial assistance"; whether regulations impose additional school requirements "remains to be seen" — [Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2025/08/federal-school-choice-program-2025-what-it-means); Holland & Knight (Oct 2026): private schools "should review applicable federal rules, state law and SGO participation requirements before accepting scholarship payments" — [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/10/treasury-department-guidance-clarifies-the-education)
- Fisher Phillips (Oct 2026): SGOs "can adopt more stringent requirements than the federal mandate", so schools "should expect that individual SGOs may add their own documentation rules" — [Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/insights/insights/proposed-federal-school-choice-regulations-what-private-schools-need-to-know)
- ACE partner-school onboarding: email schools@acescholarships.org with school name, website, state, contact email/phone, grades served; physical K-12 private schools only; slots depend on funds — [ACE help center](https://help.acescholarships.org/hc/en-us/articles/20390926669595-How-do-I-become-an-ACE-partner-school); families then apply through FACTS after enrolling — [ACE how to apply](https://help.acescholarships.org/hc/en-us/articles/21734116101275-How-to-Apply-A-Step-by-Step-Guide-on-Applying-for-an-ACE-Scholarship)
- AFC Scholarship Fund school onboarding: "a short interest form, then an eligibility check and a co-branded portal setup" (Odyssey platform); eligibility criteria not published — [AFC for schools](https://afcscholarshipfund.org/for-schools-and-sgos/)
- FACTS describes the typical cycle: families apply through the SGO, "the school confirms that a student who named the school is enrolled, with income and audit work kept by the SGO" — [FACTS blog](https://factsmgt.com/blog/how-sgos-and-schools-partner-to-expand-access-to-education/); FACTS school-choice resource page — [FACTS](https://factsmgt.com/school-choice-resource/)
- Opportunity Fund for Jewish Education: schools "can register with the fund to receive updates and begin the participation process" — [Opportunity Fund for schools](https://jewishopportunity.org/for-schools/)
- American GOAL: works with dioceses, independent schools and school associations; publishes participating schools by state — [American GOAL participating schools](https://americangoal.org/participating-schools/)
- Example of a state-level school participation agreement deadline (South Dakota: annually by 15 November) — [SD Division of Insurance](https://dlr.sd.gov/insurance/tax_credit_program/sgo_information.aspx) (state rule, not federal)
- "Partner school" marketplaces have appeared ("Join an SGO as a Partner School — $0 to Start") — [sgoguide partner schools](https://sgoguide.com/products/partner-schools); [sgoguide single school](https://sgoguide.com/single-school)
- New York school recognition: NYSED's State Office of Religious and Independent Schools "uses the Basic Educational Data System (BEDS) code to recognize the existence of religious and independent schools"; each school building needs its own BEDS code; for state reimbursement programs a nonpublic school must instruct per Education Law §3204, require full-time attendance per §3205, be a not-for-profit corporation, and "be recognized as a school by the filing of a BEDS report" — [NYSED Running a School](https://www.p12.nysed.gov/nonpub/running/); [NYSED funding opportunities](https://www.p12.nysed.gov/nonpub/fundingopportunities/); federal overview of NY private-school regulation — [ED.gov NY](https://www.ed.gov/birth-grade-12-education/education-choice/state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools/new-york-state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools)
- Under the regulations a "school" is a K-12 school as determined under state law (§530 reference) and qualified expenses are tied to enrollment or attendance — [AFC homeschool article](https://afcscholarshipfund.org/articles/eftc-homeschool-school-definition-state-law/); [Pioneer Institute FAQ](https://pioneerinstitute.org/federal-scholarship-program-faq/)
- ACE lists 31 Dec 2026 as the last day to donate for a 2027 credit (sic; donations for the 2027 credit begin 1 Jan 2027 per all other sources) — [ACE FTCS](https://www.acescholarships.org/ftcs); contributions for the credit begin 1 Jan 2027 — [CSF FSTC](https://scholarshipfund.org/federal-scholarship-tax-credit/)

### Inferences
- A Miller Place school opening September 2027 can realistically submit interest forms to ACE, AFC Scholarship Fund, CSF and American GOAL in early 2027 (none of the published onboarding steps require an operating history), but should expect any SGO's "eligibility check" to ask for proof that it is a NYSED-recognized nonpublic school (BEDS code, not-for-profit status) and probably a W-9/tuition schedule before it can be invoiced against. Nothing found suggests a school must be accredited; accreditation requirements, if any, would be SGO-specific.
- Because scholarships for 2027–28 will be awarded in 2027 from donations that only begin 1 Jan 2027 and SGO lists only finalize 15 Feb 2027, the first scholarship payments for a September 2027 opening would be late-summer/fall 2027 at the earliest — timing that fits a September 2027 opening if the school is BEDS-registered by then.

### Gaps
- No SGO publishes a policy on schools that are not yet operating; no source addresses whether an SGO's "eligibility check" can be completed before a school has a BEDS code.
- No source lists fees charged to schools, data-sharing requirements, background-check or nondiscrimination attestations for the federal program; ACE's and AFC's school pages only describe intake steps.
- NYSED's own treatment of a brand-new nonpublic school for §25F purposes is unaddressed.
