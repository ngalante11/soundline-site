# New York State and Long Island scholarship organizations positioned to become federal SGOs (IRC 25F) — candidate partners for a new non-sectarian independent middle school in Miller Place, Suffolk County

Research date: 29 October 2026. Method note: most organization websites (scholarshipfund.org, tomorrowshopefoundation.org, nyscatholic.org, thegoodnewsroom.org, teachcoalition.org, jewishopportunity.org, longislandpress.com, chalkbeat.org, pkfod.com, eftccredit.com, sgocheck.com, sgoguide.com) could not be fetched directly from this session (blocked network domains), so findings below rely on search-engine extracts of those pages plus two IRS pages that were fetched in full. Items labeled "[inferred]" are my inference, not a confirmed statement by the organization. Contact details are recorded as found in search extracts and should be re-verified before outreach.

## Context: where New York stands on the federal credit (as of 29 Oct 2026)

### Takeaway
Gov. Hochul publicly announced on 9 October 2026 that New York will opt in to the federal scholarship tax credit for 2027, but as of the IRS's 22 October 2026 update New York did NOT yet appear on the IRS list of 30 states that have filed an advance election, and no New York-approved SGO list has been published. The state agency/process by which New York will certify SGOs has not been publicly identified in any source I found.

### Cited Findings
- Hochul announced 9 Oct 2026 that New York will opt into the Federal Scholarship Tax Credit, making her the second Democratic governor (after Colorado's Polis) to do so after Treasury published regulations — [Chalkbeat, 9 Oct 2026](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/10/09/ny-gov-hochul-opts-into-federal-scholarship-tax-credit/); [Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/10/hochul-will-opt-new-york-into-federal-scholarship-tax-credit-program-that-will-help-fund-jewish-education/); [PKF O'Connor Davies](https://www.pkfod.com/insights/new-york-will-officially-opt-into-the-federal-scholarship-tax-credit-program/)

- A third-party tracker (eftccredit.com) counts New York as the 31st state "that has said it will participate," distinguishing it from the 30 on the official IRS list — [eftccredit.com SGO directory](https://eftccredit.com/sgos) (search extract; conflicts with the IRS list only in that NY's filing is not yet reflected)
- Federal mechanics for states: advance election on Form 15714 due on or before 1 Jan 2027; the election must be "perfected" by submitting the state's SGO list on or before 15 Feb 2027 — [eftccredit.com federal SGO list explainer](https://eftccredit.com/learn/federal-sgo-list); IRS page confirms states must submit an annual SGO list via the "FSTC State Portal" and SGOs must "ask to be included on a participating state's SGO list" and register via the "FSTC SGO Portal" — [IRS FSTC page (fetched)](https://www.irs.gov/government-entities/federal-state-local-governments/federal-scholarship-tax-credit-fstc)
- SGO requirements per IRS/Treasury: 501(c)(3) public charity (not a private foundation), located in the participating state, segregated accounting for scholarship funds, student income verification (household income not above 300% of area median gross income), spend at least 90% of income on scholarships, award scholarships to at least 10 students who do not all attend the same school, prioritize prior-year recipients and their siblings — [IRS FSTC page (fetched)](https://www.irs.gov/government-entities/federal-state-local-governments/federal-scholarship-tax-credit-fstc); [NYSSCPA, 20 Oct 2026](https://nysscpa.org/news/9100817-scholarship-organizations-urged-to-prepare-for-new-tax-credit-requirements-2026-10-20); [Federal Register proposed regs, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/02/2026-20277/federal-scholarship-tax-credit)
- Temporary regs allow a state to list an organization whose 501(c)(3) determination is still pending, if the organization is formed before 1 Jan 2027 — [eftccredit.com "How to start an SGO"](https://eftccredit.com/learn/how-to-start-an-sgo)
- NYSSCPA reports practitioners advising that "some organizations are establishing separate entities to comply with the spending requirements" (the 90% rule) — [NYSSCPA](https://nysscpa.org/news/9100817-scholarship-organizations-urged-to-prepare-for-new-tax-credit-requirements-2026-10-20)
- Opposition: NYSUT says it will keep working to repeal the program federally; State Sen. - Long Island public-school angle: Hicksville Board of Education discussed the SGO program at its 21 Oct 2026 meeting; Superintendent Theodore Fulton said the district will share SGO regulations and timelines when available — [Long Island Press, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.longislandpress.com/2026/10/26/hicksville-board-of-education-optimistic-about-scholarship-program-offering-benefits-for-students-and-tax-breaks-for-donors/)

### Inferences
- [inferred] Because NY is not yet on the IRS list, any New York organization's status as an "approved SGO" is at this point self-declared intent, not certification. The first binding list will be the one NY submits by 15 Feb 2027.
- [inferred] A brand-new school opening after 2027 would need to be accepted by an SGO that NY has listed; the school itself does not register with the IRS or the state.

### Gaps
- No source identifies which New York agency (Division of the Budget, Tax & Finance, SED, or the Executive Chamber) will receive SGO applications, or the application form/criteria. The Governor's own press release text was not retrievable.
- No New York SGO list has been published anywhere I could find.

## Key Question 1: Which New York organizations have publicly said they intend to register as federal SGOs for 2027 (or are forming new ones)?

### Takeaway
Publicly identified New York SGO candidates fall into three camps: (a) the Catholic diocesan funds that the NYS Catholic Conference lists as "your diocese's SGO" — Inner-City Scholarship Fund (Archdiocese of NY), Futures in Education (Brooklyn/Queens), and an unnamed Rockville Centre SGO that is almost certainly Tomorrow's Hope Foundation; (b) Children's Scholarship Fund (CSF), which describes itself as an SGO and sells "SGO services" to other organizations; and (c) new Jewish-community and national SGOs (Opportunity Fund for Jewish Education; AFC Scholarship Fund) that will operate in New York. No independent-school (NYSAIS-type) or Long Island community-foundation SGO has been announced.

## Key Question 3: Which organizations would plausibly serve a non-religious, independent, newly founded school in Suffolk County? Do any have an "open to any accredited/registered private school" policy?

### Takeaway
Only CSF (nationally) and the AFC Scholarship Fund state school-agnostic policies; but CSF New York's own program uses a closed list of schools "designated by our funding partners," and its Long Island partner (Tomorrow's Hope) is Catholic-only by charter. ICSF, Futures in Education and the Opportunity Fund are limited to their own networks. There is currently no Long Island SGO with an open-school policy.

### Cited Findings
- CSF national policy: scholarships can be used at non-religious, Catholic, non-Catholic Christian, Jewish, and Islamic schools; "the school may be parochial, denominational, independent, or a home school"; "a scholarship can go to any legally operating private school" in most locations — [CSF FAQ](https://scholarshipfund.org/apply/faq/) (search extract)
- CSF New York exception: "in a few cities, including New York, CSF families choose from a list of participating schools that are designated by our funding partners" — [CSF FAQ](https://scholarshipfund.org/apply/faq/)
- CSF New York eligibility: K-8 students living in the five boroughs; income based on National School Lunch Program scale; awards up to 75% of tuition, max about $3,600/child/year (2026-27 per Mathnasium page) — [Mathnasium CSF page](https://www.mathnasium.com/new-york-city-csf-scholarship); [CSF NYC links](https://nyc.scholarshipfund.org/links/)
- CSF New York does partner with non-Catholic schools, e.g., Al-Madinah School (Islamic) posts CSF application instructions — [Al-Madinah School](https://www.almadinah-school.com/apps/news/article/1914241)

- Tomorrow's Hope: eligibility limited to "a Catholic elementary school on Long Island" — [THF FAQ](http://www.tomorrowshopefoundation.org/frequently-asked-questions/); DRVC describes it as "set up exclusively to support families wishing to send their child(ren) to Catholic elementary school" — [DRVC Schools FAQ](https://drvcschools.org/frequently-asked-questions)
- ICSF: serves students attending "schools in the Archdiocese of New York" (about 40% of its students are non-Catholic, but the schools are archdiocesan) — [ICSF home](https://www.innercityscholarshipfund.org/)

- AFC Scholarship Fund: funds go to "the educational program that fits their family — not to specific institutions"; federal rules allow private and religious schools, tutoring, books — [AFC Scholarship Fund SGO explainer](https://afcscholarshipfund.org/articles/what-is-a-scholarship-granting-organization-sgo/)

## Key Question 4: What partnership/application processes do these organizations already run for schools, and what do they ask of a school?

### Takeaway
The documented processes are family-facing (FACTS-based need verification, annual application windows). School-facing onboarding is described only by CSF (SGO Services; partner-school database/forms in affiliates) and the Opportunity Fund (web registration form with streamlined paths for affiliated schools). ICSF publishes a school-administrator "reference tool," suggesting an annual school-coordinator workflow.

### Cited Findings
- CSF SGO Services: two models — CSF advises an organization running its own SGO, or CSF acts as the SGO "handling administration and compliance"; eligible partners include private schools, dioceses, school networks, scholarship organizations and other nonprofits; no form/deadline shown — [CSF SGO Services](https://scholarshipfund.org/sgoservices/)
- CSF family process: find nearest partner program, confirm it is offering new scholarships and basic requirements, submit federal tax forms and other financial information plus a current public-school report card (except incoming kindergartners) — [CSF Apply](https://scholarshipfund.org/apply/)
- CSF New York family process: online pre-application, then invitation to full application with financial documents; annual open-enrollment window — [Al-Madinah School notice](https://www.almadinah-school.com/apps/news/article/1914241); [CSF FAQ](https://scholarshipfund.org/apply/faq/)
- CSF Philadelphia affiliate (illustrative of CSF partner-school mechanics): 150+ partner schools; "designated school personnel may complete all of CSFP's required forms as well as track CSFP families and payments through its shared database" — [CSF Philadelphia FAQs](https://www.csfphiladelphia.org/scholarships/faqs/)
- Tomorrow's Hope: FACTS Grant & Aid processes applications and verifies financial documents; Foundation reviews completed applications; K-8; deadline April 30 (may reopen if funds remain); first-come-first-served per school pages — [THF FAQ](http://www.tomorrowshopefoundation.org/frequently-asked-questions/); [St. Patrick School Bay Shore](https://spsbayshore.org/news/tomorrows-hope-scholarship-opportunity); [St. Brigid/OLH Westbury](https://stbolh.org/news/tomorrowaposs-hope-foundation-tuition-assistance-application)
- ICSF: publishes a "School Administrators Reference Tool" for school staff each year (2024-25 edition found), indicating a school-coordinator role in the award process — [ICSF Reference Tool PDF](https://www.innercityscholarshipfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2024-2025-School-Administrators-Reference-Tool-for-Toolbox.pdf)
- Opportunity Fund for Jewish Education: schools register via web form; schools affiliated with Prizmah, Torah Umesorah, Teach Coalition or Agudath Israel get a "streamlined path," unaffiliated schools go through an onboarding process; families apply directly to the Fund; participating schools receive materials to share — [Opportunity Fund — For Schools](https://jewishopportunity.org/for-schools/)
- Federal verification SGOs must perform on behalf of schools' families: household income not above 300% of area median gross income; student eligible to enroll in a public elementary/secondary school; priority to prior recipients and siblings — [Federal Register proposed regs](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/02/2026-20277/federal-scholarship-tax-credit); [Queens Chronicle](https://www.qchron.com/editions/queenswide/state-opts-in-to-federal-scholarship-tax-credit/article_b661fea8-c95f-5c2f-b44b-e0a5d36e044d.html)

### Inferences
- [inferred] A new school approaching CSF should expect to show legal operating status (NYSED registration/BEDS code), a tuition schedule, a designated financial-aid coordinator, and willingness to use the SGO's shared database and FACTS-type verification.
- [inferred] Because awards are paid to schools on behalf of students, SGOs will want a bank/vendor setup and enrollment confirmation from the school before disbursing.

### Gaps
- No organization's written "school partner agreement" or onboarding checklist was retrievable.
