Our vision

By eighth grade, a student who can:

  1. 01

    Learn something hard.

    And prove it.

  2. 02

    Make something new.

    For a client in town, on a deadline.

  3. 03

    Think it through.

    In writing, first.

  4. 04

    Say it out loud.

    And defend it, no screen.

  5. 05

    Show up for their town.

    One project a year is for the town.

  6. 06

    Know where they're headed.

    And how they got there.

New York just decided a diploma should mean six things, not a set of test scores. We built the school on those six.

Every six weeks, your child adds to a folder of proof: made, defended, grown. By eighth grade it's a portfolio.

How you'll know it's working.

We measure growth three times a year and publish it.

NWEA MAP, three times a year. We publish whole-school growth, counting everyone. No child's score is public; yours comes home.

You'll see the work.

The robot, the performance, your child explaining it.

AI is a subject here.

Taught on purpose. Never a shortcut in seminar, lab, or the defense.

We answer to New York.

We register with the state, the district reviews our program, and accreditation starts once we're open.

Why it works

None of this is new. Putting it in one building is.

Mornings

You don't move on until you've got it.

Mastery before moving on beats the calendar, in over a hundred studies. Struggling students gain most.

Afternoons

Real projects raise real scores.

In randomized trials, long projects beat the usual way on state tests.

Seminar

Writing it down is how it sticks.

Writing about it, and saying it out loud, is how it sticks.

Advisory

Being known changes how hard a student works.

One close teacher relationship is among the strongest predictors of effort, more so with age. Hence one advisor for three years.
