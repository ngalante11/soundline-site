What’s included

Everything in the school day: classes, science labs, projects, art and music. There is no materials fee.

Extended Day

After school, 3:00 to 5:30, for five-day students. It is $330 a month over twelve months, or $297 for Founding Families. You pay only if you use it.

Two kids

The second child is 10% off.

Class size

Ten to twelve students in a grade. One adult stays with your child for all three years.

Paying in full

Pay for the whole year by March 1 and take another 3% off.

No application fee

It costs nothing to apply. Anything you pay now counts toward tuition.

What you pay, and when.

There are no extra fees. Each payment counts toward the next one.

  1. $500

    Holds a seat.

    It is kept by an independent escrow agent, not by us. You get all of it back, for any reason, until the day we sign a lease.

  2. $2,500

    Your deposit.

    This is due when you sign the enrollment contract. Your $500 counts toward it. You will know the school’s address before we ask for it.

  3. Tuition

    Twelve monthly payments.

    Your deposit counts toward tuition. Monthly payments start the summer before school opens. You do not owe two big payments in August and January.

Questions parents ask.

Money
What is a Founding Family?

One of the first families to join. Founding Families pay 10% less, and the price they start at is the price they pay every year their child is here, through twelfth grade. It does not go up when our costs go up, and it does not go up when the school fills.

How is it paid?

Monthly, over twelve months, starting the summer before school opens. You do not owe two big payments in August and January.

What if the school doesn’t open?

You get your money back. Your $500 comes back in full, for any reason, until the day we sign a lease. If the school doesn’t open after that, we mail you a check.

Is there financial aid of your own?

Yes, and it is a real line in our first budget rather than an afterthought. Tell us that cost is the question for you. It changes how we build the aid pool, and it does not count against your child.

Is the bus really free?

In most cases, yes. Your own district buses students to private schools up to fifteen miles from home, and the district pays for it. You have to ask by April 1. We send you the form already filled in.

The school
How many students will there be?

Ten to twelve in a grade. Nobody is stuck in their grade, because students work at their own level across the school. On Fridays the whole school builds one thing together. It is few enough that no child gets overlooked, and enough for a real class discussion.

Where will the school be?

On the North Shore, along the Miller Place–Port Jefferson Station corridor. Port Jefferson Station is the likeliest. We announce the address the day we sign the lease.

What about high school?

Ninth grade opens here in September 2028. Then we add a grade every year through twelfth. New York has decided a diploma should stand for six things a graduate can do, not only test scores. Your child practices all six here, every six weeks.

How much screen time is there?

Very little. Morning math, reading and writing are hands-on, at a table with a guide (our word for a teacher). The only screen in the morning belongs to the guide. Class discussions, science lab and presentations happen without screens. AI is taught as a subject. It is never used as a shortcut.

New York
Is Soundline approved by New York?

New York does not license private schools. A private school registers with the state, and the local district reviews what it teaches. We do both. We are not accredited yet. We start that process once we are open.

Do students take the state tests?

Yes. The state tests are in the spring and take three mornings. We do not do test prep. New York uses the tests to check that a new school is teaching what it should. All year long you will also see how much your own child has grown, three times a year.

How do you screen the adults who work with my child?

Every adult who works with students is fingerprinted through the New York State Education Department before they start, and is checked against the sex offender registry. Everyone completes mandated reporter training. There are always at least two adults in the building.

My child has an IEP. What happens to services?

Your child can still receive services from the public school district. You request them from the district where the school is located by June 1, and we’ll help you do it.

A federal scholarship starts the January before we open.

A new federal tax credit for private school scholarships begins January 1, 2027. New York has said it will opt in and has not yet filed. We have written up everything we know.

Is your child younger, or older?

We open in September 2027 with grades 6, 7 and 8. If the timing is not right for your child, tell us, and we will call you when it is.

  • Younger than sixth grade.

    A new sixth grade starts every September. Tell us what grade your child is in now, and we will call you before sign-up opens for their year.

  • Thinking about a later year.

    Tell us the year you have in mind. We will call you before sign-up opens, and tell you whether your child’s grade has a seat.

  • High school.

    Ninth grade opens in September 2028, and we add a grade every year after that, through twelfth. That means we will not have a grade for students who are in eighth grade or higher this school year.
